# Authors

To see the list of PortaNode authors for copyright purposes, see the
revision history in source control:
<https://github.com/btclib-org/portanode/graphs/contributors>

The binaries this folder launches are not among them. Bitcoin Core and
Electrum are somebody else's work, released under their own licences, and
they are not in this tree at all — `macos/bin/.gitignore` and
`win/bin/.gitignore` name them one by one, and the update scripts fetch
them from the publisher and verify the publisher's signature before
installing. What is authored here is the launchers, the update and
verification scripts, and the prose around them.
